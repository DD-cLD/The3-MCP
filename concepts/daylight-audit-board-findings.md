**Structural finding:** the **opener block plays at PEAK sun in every daylight show** (the closer block, by contrast, always gets the best light — dusk/dark). This makes any night-architected opener board a **daylight liability by construction**, independent of that board's own color choices.

**#1 daylight liability: Genius** — night-architected, sits in the peak-sun opener block every show.

**Build-priority order** (worst/most urgent first): **Genius → SONG_H → SONG_Q (SONG_Q) → SONG_L → song-g-ending.**

**Per-board ratings:**
- **Most daylight-native of the ten boards: From Scratch** — built from Warm White + Amber only, already legible in daylight by construction.
- **Most hostile: SONG_L** — saturated ice-blue field identity, the opposite of the white-frame daytime vocabulary (see `tourshow-daylight-design-vocabulary`).
- **SONG_B (unbuilt)** should be designed **daylight-FIRST** when it's eventually built — it sits in the peak-sun opener block every show, same structural liability as Genius.
