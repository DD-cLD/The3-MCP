**The question Dave asked:** *"how do findings get PULLED when needed — turn them into skills?"*

**The ruling.** **FACTS STAY CONCEPTS** — 281 skills would just rebuild the haystack in a different shape. **Recurring TASK-SHAPES become harness-level trigger skills** that POINT at repo briefs and manifests, carrying **zero duplicated content**. That last property is the point: a skill that holds no content cannot drift stale against the repo.

**What makes something skill-shaped:** a **stable procedure with variable content**. The song build qualifies — same five steps every time, a different song each time. `/kickoff` is the proven instance: same boot, different state. Creative translation is *not* skill-shaped. Variability in the *content* is fine; variability in the *procedure* is what disqualifies.

**Four drafted and delivered for Dave's save** (source copies in `WORKING/agents/doorway_skills/`): `ma3-macro-gate`, `ma3-sequence-tc-gate`, `ma3-phaser-gate`, `ma3-desk-session`.

**THE FOUR RETRIEVAL RINGS — judgment decreasing at each step:**

1. **Index map** — skim and decide. Maximum judgment; this is the ring that **failed** in the quote-bug retrieval miss.
2. **Doc-bound arming manifests** — the document that owns a step names the required reading. Less judgment: the doc tells you.
3. **Smith boots** — the manifest IS the boot. No judgment: a scoped agent cannot start without reading it.
4. **Harness skill triggers** — fire on the task-shape phrase, **before the session has decided anything**. This is the only ring that can arm ahead of a wrong decision.

Even unsaved as harness skills, rings 1–3 hold via README and the repo.

**Why the ordering matters:** the ring that actually prevented failure in practice was ring 3, and the reason was **not** its dialect cut — it was that **dispatch is a boundary that structurally forces arming**. Any mechanism that creates a hard boundary at a territory change inherits that property. Conversely, rings that rely on the session choosing to look (ring 1) fail exactly when a session is absorbed in another territory — which is precisely when it needs them.

**Corollary for skills specifically:** put a skill wherever a *task-type transition* happens, because transitions are where re-arming gets skipped.
