**Proposed doctrine (from the daylight audit, not yet ratified into a versioned design-theory doc):** in daylight conditions, **key-only lighting IS the functional equivalent of a blackout.**

**The corollary:** every dark gesture in the existing vocabulary — **voids, black cuts, dark holds, silhouettes, floor-alone** — translates to **"contrast-to-her"** (i.e., the daylight-legible substitute for a dark gesture is dropping everything to key light on {ARTIST}, creating contrast against the ambient daylight, rather than trying to achieve actual darkness). **One corollary is proposed to cover the entire dark-gesture inventory** at once, rather than needing a bespoke daylight substitute per gesture type.

**Status:** flagged as a candidate line for **`TOUR_DESIGN_THEORY_v0.3`** — not yet written into that doc, not yet ratified by Dave.
