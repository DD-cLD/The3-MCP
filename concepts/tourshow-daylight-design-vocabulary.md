**Status change (2026-07-08):** now that the EU festival leg's actual show times are known (see `tourshow-eu-sun-and-venue-ledger`) and turn out to be **mostly daytime** (only {FESTIVAL} definite-night, a couple dusky), `TOUR_DESIGN_THEORY_v0.2.md`'s daylight doctrine goes from theoretical to **operative** — it governs the majority of EU shows, not a hypothetical edge case.

**The vocabulary:**
- **Key-her-first** — {ARTIST}'s key light/position takes priority over other design moves in daylight conditions.
- **Authored-silhouettes-or-none** — a silhouette against a bright background must be a deliberate design choice, never a default (see `law-zero-and-failover-architecture`'s followspot doctrine — the {FESTIVAL} "SONG_B" tape is the cited evidence that upstage blocking + daylight + bright wall = accidental silhouette without a followspot).
- **White-frame daytime vocabulary** — the frame-white families (Neutral/Warm/Cool White per `tourshow-palette-and-groove-v2`) are the primary legible tool in daylight; punch colors read poorly against sun.
- **Saturate fields = dusk-and-after** — fully saturated color fields are reserved for dusk-crossing and night shows ({FESTIVAL}, {FESTIVAL}, and the Aug 22–23 tent pair), where they'll actually read.

**New work this activates:** a per-show sun-ledger cross-check + a daylight-readiness audit of the existing 10 Bird's Eye boards (do they hold up under the white-frame daytime vocabulary, or were they designed assuming night?) — tracked as a work item, not yet done.
