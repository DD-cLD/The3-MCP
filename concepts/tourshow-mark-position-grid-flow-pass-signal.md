**L7 Mark-cue position grid built** (`generated/desk_pack/MARK_CUE_POSITION_GRID.csv`): 156 rows, 16 songs, 10 distinct Mark-cue positions show-wide. (Selector gotcha paid to get a trustworthy census here: `empty-census-deserves-selector-suspicion`.)

**The flow-pass signal is already visible in the census itself: JDC sits at `Mid` and QX40 at `Lowest` in EVERY SINGLE SONG** — both fixture groups are static show-wide at Mark. SPOTS, by contrast, already varies (Lowest / Down / DSC / High / FAN / I I across songs).

**Reading:** the two obvious candidates for varying position across the set are **JDC and QX40**, not spots — spots are already doing that work. Static-at-Mark is not the same as static-throughout-the-song, but it is the show-wide starting-position signal, and it points at the two groups that currently carry no set-to-set variation.
