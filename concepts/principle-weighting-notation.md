**Pointer concept — the doc is the source, read it there, not here.**
`WORKING/PRINCIPLE_WEIGHTING_NOTATION.md` defines a density-weighted notation for authoring
dense text: `[Symbol] G=[value] | [SCOPE] — [Domain]`, where ◼/◆/○ signal load-bearing /
structural / flexible and G=1.0→0.1 sets gravitational weight (Bedrock down to Suggestion) —
under one unbreakable rule: **higher G = fewer words** (density inversion; hard cap 3-5
bedrock principles per document).

**Why operational-meta, not spine:** this governs how cLD *authors the corpus* (e.g. the
[0803-3cLD] INBOX block filed alongside this concept), not how it programs a console — it
never rides `SPINE.md`.

**Digest rule this concept enforces on the librarian:** when filing a weighted finding,
preserve its weight in the concept body's tone (a G=0.9 line reads as a hard rule; a G=0.3
line reads as a soft preference) — don't flatten to equal-sounding prose, and don't pad a
terse high-G line back out to match a longer house-style paragraph.

Read the doc for: the full symbol/G-value tables, the `[WHEN: condition]` / `[ECHO: ...]`
mechanisms, equal-G collision (narrowest scope wins), and the rejected mechanics (negative
gravity, mass budgets, meta-preambles).
