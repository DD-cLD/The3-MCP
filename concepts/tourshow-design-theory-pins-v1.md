From `TOUR_DESIGN_THEORY_v0.1.md` — explicitly **laws NOT yet ratified** at time of writing (see `tourshow-palette-and-groove-v2` for the v0.2 pass, which does ratify Law 0 and revises several of these):

- **Ground↔air axis:** warm ground (amber/peach/coral/straw = skin/love/voice → floor package) vs. cool air (teal/lavender/plum/indigo = sky/distance/night → house rig). Tiers and poles are the same idea.
- **Master palette (8 anchors)** + **one signature color per song max**. Named examples: Genius: Haze Blue #7FA8C9 · SONG_T: Duet Rose #C9527E · SONG_G: Ember #C05A2E.
- **Working device set across 4 boards:** Isolate, Sustain drift, Bloom, Phrase turn, Color pump, Propagation, Impact (+ Void as punctuation). **Riser/Chase/Strobe burst: 0 of 4 boards** — the framing given is "soul music, not EDM."
- **Three fixed show images:** Genius 0:55 reveal (first full-rig image) · SONG_T golden peak · **SONG_G floor-alone ember** (house rig exits tier-by-tier in the 87 s dissolve; the show ends on the floor package alone — "the floor-alone principle performed nightly").

Note: `tourshow-palette-and-groove-v2` later **kills** the SONG_T "Duet Rose" signature color and the "golden peak" image, and **strikes** a "no cold white" law that doesn't appear stated in this v0.1 pin list but is referenced as struck in v0.2 — treat this file as the v0.1 starting point, not current design law where the two conflict.
