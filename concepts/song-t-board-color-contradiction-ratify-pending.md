**⚑ THE LESSON THIS COST — PROVENANCE DETERMINES AUTHORITY.** The corpus held the board and
the white-void call as two peer authorities needing adjudication, *even though it had
already recorded that the board predated the white-void call* (see the original body
below). What was missing was that the board was **self-generated ideation**, not an
external design input. **Before treating two artifacts as contradictory, establish who made
each one, when, and on what information.** An early self-generated sketch is not evidence
against later informed intent. Pairs with the same-week lesson that on an inherited file
**membership is ground truth and names/labels are hypotheses** — both are cases of trusting
an artifact's label instead of its provenance.
