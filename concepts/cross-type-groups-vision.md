Dave's stated design vision, dictated at the close of the 2026-07-14 grid-drill session: **cross-type grid groups are a signature move** — building ONE grid group that mixes different fixture types, rather than grouping and chasing within a single type ("not just spots chasing with spots").

The payoff is rhythmic: mixing types on one grid lets a chase produce rhythm-ride figures that alternate or interleave across types — Dave's coined shorthand for this feel is **"boom-boom-pop."** These are complex, interesting-looking groups that are rarely built, since most rigs group strictly by type.

Groups 123 ("cLD DS WING PAIRED16" — mirrored pairing) and 124 ("cLD DS WING BOUNCE" — alternating-column bounce) are the concrete machinery that makes this vision buildable: pairing puts two types in sync on shared columns, bouncing alternates them step by step. Both are cross-type by construction.
